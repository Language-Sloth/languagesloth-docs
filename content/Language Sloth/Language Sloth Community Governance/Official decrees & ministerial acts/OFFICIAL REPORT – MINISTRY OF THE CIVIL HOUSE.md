Khanate – Server
Subject: Consolidated Report on Conduct and Violations by Administrator @Anthony (SLTH)
Date: May 24, 2026
Prepared by: Minister Marescallus Zanella (Biaggioni) – Minister of the Civil House of Internal Relations

Dear Sirs,
This report consolidates all irregularities identified in the conduct of administrator @Anthony, based on the provided prints and information, the official server rules, and the competencies established by the Khanate Decree. Each error is presented clearly, with a description of what occurred and the corresponding violation.
1. Error 1 – Refusal to provide accountability and lack of transparency
@Anthony was questioned multiple times about his actions and plans but repeatedly replied that he would prefer not to answer. He claimed to be conducting audits and writing plans without ever presenting any report. This conduct violates the server’s Respect rule and disregards the hierarchical structure established by the Khanate Decree.
2. Error 2 – Hostile, curt attitude and creation of drama
When questioned, @Anthony adopted a defensive and unprofessional tone, using phrases such as “Am I not going to go back and forth” and “That don’t bother me.” Instead of resolving conflicts, his attitude increased tension, violating the Save the Drama for Your Mama rule.
3. Error 3 – Disregard for the work and merit of other team members
@Anthony publicly belittled the work of Pengu, who had the highest numbers in the mod-panel. This attitude violates the spirit of the Respect rule.
4. Error 4 – Independent action and breach of hierarchy
@Anthony made moderation, ban, and region assignment decisions without any coordination with the Minister of the Civil House or consultation with other administrators and ministers.
5. Error 5 – Lack of professionalism and inadequate communication The administrator used informal language and a superior tone when questioned, showing resistance to dialogue. 
6. Error 6 – Lack of collaboration with the team @Anthony showed resistance to answering legitimate questions and preferred to abruptly end discussions. 
7. Error 7 – Undue interference in the Ministry of the Civil House and failure to consult peers Even without holding a defined ministry, @Anthony repeatedly interfered in the attributions of the Ministry of the Civil House and Internal Affairs, which is responsible for mediating conflicts, appointing Captains and Moderators together with the ministers, and removing them when necessary. The Ministry does not carry out appointments or removals without consulting its peers. 
8. Error 8 – Unjust demotion of a highly active Captain Moderator @Anthony demoted a Captain Moderator who, in recent weeks, had been by far the most active member of the team in voice chat minutes, evidence posted, mutes, unmutes, bans, and cases solved. When questioned about the demotion, he reduced the entire discussion to a message from two hours earlier and accused the former Captain of “causing drama” and being “hostile.” When asked about the proportionality of his decision, he replied “that don’t bother me,” showing complete disregard for the current law and hierarchy system. 
9. Error 9 – Lack of respect and interest in the current system @Anthony explicitly demonstrated lack of knowledge and interest in learning and adapting to the current system. He made statements such as “Berke was an admin”, “I only care about the server, not berke’s system”, and “my loyalty is to sloth not berke.” These declarations clearly show that he neither respects nor intends to follow the organizational system currently in place in the Khanate.
10. Error 10 – Promotion without transparency and refusal to explain attributions @Anthony promoted the user “Wolfarino” to Captain Moderator but refused to explain what his regional captaincy would be. This information is a central part of the Captain Moderator role, which exists precisely to be responsible for a specific region of moderators. The refusal to clarify weakens the structure and creates confusion regarding responsibilities.
11. Error 11 – Dictatorial behavior and imposition on other moderators When questioned about his decisions and plans, @Anthony showed weakness by being overly imposing, dictating the behavior of other moderators (who are not his direct employees) by saying he would prefer they did not ask questions, as “there is more important stuff at hand than asking me questions, I already gave you guys the basics.” 
12. Error 12 – Failure to consult other administrators (“dictatorship” behavior) When other administrators were contacted about @Anthony’s behavior, none of them confirmed that he had spoken with them about his plans for changes in the current system. This demonstrates a pattern of acting in isolation and imposing decisions without consulting his peers, configuring authoritarian behavior among equals. 
13. Error 13 – Criticism of the system without understanding correct responsibilities @Anthony stated that parts of the current system are “only useful on paper.” When detailing his criticisms, he made serious mistakes regarding role responsibilities: inactivity of titled members and lack of moderator training are responsibilities of the Captain Moderators; lack of attached evidence and case confusion are responsibilities of the Senate. Only one of his criticisms (lack of clarity about who is responsible for what) is pertinent and falls under administrative competence. The others demonstrate a lack of understanding of the current responsibility structure.

General Impact @Anthony’s conduct has generated an atmosphere of tension, distrust, and demotivation among team members. In addition to violating specific server rules, his actions weaken the organizational structure created by the Khanate, compromise the functioning of the Ministry of the Civil House, disrespect the established hierarchy, and create a dangerous precedent of unilateral and authoritarian decisions made without consultation or transparency. 

Recommendations
It is recommended that an administrative inquiry process be immediately initiated to thoroughly investigate all the facts reported herein and to formally gather the opinions of the other administrators and the current Server Owner, His Excellency Cosmos (as the Great Khan is currently away). This process should aim to determine the appropriate measures regarding @Anthony’s conduct and to reinforce the hierarchical structure and collaborative decision-making principles of the Khanate.
It is further recommended that @Anthony be requested to immediately submit a complete report of all actions taken since assuming his position, and that his decision-making autonomy be temporarily suspended, especially concerning appointments, demotions, and changes to the current system, until the conclusion of the aforementioned inquiry.
This report was prepared based on the available evidence, server rules, and the Khanate Decree, with the objective of preserving order, respect, and the proper functioning of the team.
