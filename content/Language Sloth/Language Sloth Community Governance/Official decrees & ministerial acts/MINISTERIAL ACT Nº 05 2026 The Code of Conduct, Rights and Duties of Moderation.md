MINISTERIAL ACT Nº 05/2026 (With Immediate Effect)
The Minister of Civil Affairs and Internal Relations, in use of the powers granted
by Khanate Decree Nº 001/2026, Article 4,
MAKES KNOWN to all Moderators, Captain Moderators and Staff members:
Art. 1º – The Code of Conduct, Rights and Duties of Moderation of Language
Sloth is hereby approved and instituted.
CODE OF CONDUCT, RIGHTS AND DUTIES OF MODERATION
Chapter I – General Principles
Art. 2º – The Moderator acts as a Sheriff of the community: maintains order, deescalates conflicts, enforces rules at a surface level and leads by example.
Art. 3º – The Moderator is not a judge. Their role is to act preventively and with
good sense, escalating serious cases to higher levels.
Chapter II – Tools and Procedures
Art. 4º – Available tools for Moderators:
• Warn — Command: x!warn ID (reason)
• Mute — Command: x!mute ID (reason)
• Timeout — Command: x!timeout ID (reason)
• Ban — Command: x!ban ID (reason)
§ 1º – The Ban tool must be used with extreme caution, as it is a high-impact
measure. § 2º – Although the Moderator may execute the command, final
approval must come from Administrators or higher levels. § 3º – All moderation
actions must be evidenced in the #evidencias channel, with full record of the
infraction and the command used.
Chapter III – Captain Moderators
Art. 5º – Regional Captain Moderators are responsible for coordinating,
supervising and leading a specific group of Moderators in their designated region.
Art. 6º – Duties of Captain Moderators:
• Coordinate the team of Moderators under their responsibility;
• Receive and analyze weekly reports;
• Guide, train and give feedback;
• Resolve minor internal disputes;
• Forward serious cases to Administrators or Ministers.
Chapter IV – Moderation Hierarchy
Art. 7º – The operational hierarchy of Moderation is as follows:
Khan → Chancellor → Ministers/Administrators → Regional Captain Moderators
→ Moderators
Art. 8º – The Senate does not form part of this chain of command. The Senate
acts as an Independent Review House of the conduct of Moderators (the “police”
of the community), functioning in a separate and honorable manner, with the aim
of ensuring balance, justice and the proper functioning of the Khanate’s
institutions.
Chapter V – Final Provisions
Art. 9º – Violation of this Code may result in warning, performance review or
impeachment representation in the Senate.
Art. 10º – This Code enters into force immediately on the date of its publication
and revokes any previous incompatible provisions.
Let it be published and complied with.
Done on May 18, 2026.
