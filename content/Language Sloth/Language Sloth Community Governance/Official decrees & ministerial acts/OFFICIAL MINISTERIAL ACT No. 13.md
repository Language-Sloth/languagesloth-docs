Ministry of the Civil House of Internal Relations 
Khanate – Server
Subject: Creation of the General Moderation Captaincy and Appointment of its Captain

Date: May 30, 2026
By the authority vested in me as Minister of the Civil House of Internal Relations, and in accordance with the organizational structure established by the Khanate Decree, I hereby decree the following: 
Article 1 – Creation of the General Moderation Captaincy
The General Moderation Captaincy is hereby created, with the purpose of serving as a support structure for the server’s moderation.
Article 2 – Nature and Purpose
The General Moderation Captaincy does not constitute a regional captaincy. Its main function is to supplement the work of the Regional Captain Moderators by assisting Moderators who, at any given moment, do not have their respective direct superior (Regional Captain) available. This Captaincy exists to cover occasional gaps that, from time to time and due to unforeseen circumstances or force majeure, cannot be immediately addressed between a Captain and a Moderator of their region.
Article 3 – Hierarchy
The General Moderation Captaincy holds equal hierarchy to the other Regional Captaincies and Captain Moderators already established. Its Captain does not have authority over the Regional Captains and shall act in a complementary and supportive role, not as a substitute.
Article 4 – Appointment
Moderator @Menma is hereby appointed as Captain Moderator General, responsible for leading the General Moderation Captaincy.
Article 5 – Duties of the Captain Moderator General
The Captain Moderator General shall be responsible for:

Assisting Moderators who are temporarily without a direct Regional Captain; 
Acting in a complementary manner when Regional Captains are momentarily unavailable; Maintaining communication with the Ministry of the Civil House of Internal Relations regarding matters handled; 
Contributing to the maintenance of order and the moderation workflow within the server.

Article 6 – Term This Ministerial Act enters into force immediately upon publication and shall remain in effect until otherwise decided by the Ministry of the Civil House or the Server Owner.
