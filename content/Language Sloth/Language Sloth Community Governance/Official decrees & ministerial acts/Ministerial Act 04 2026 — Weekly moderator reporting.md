## Ministerial Act No. 04/2026 (Immediate effect)

The Minister of Civil Affairs and Internal Relations, in use of the powers granted by Khanate Decree No. 001/2026 (Article 4), **MAKES KNOWN** to all Moderators and Captain Moderators:

- **Art. 1** — After each weekly update of the activity panel, every Moderator may report directly to their respective Regional Captain Moderator. Although not mandatory, it is strongly recommended that they do so weekly in order to:
    - Maintain the good functioning of moderation
    - Enable quick feedback
    - Identify problems early
    - Strengthen teamwork
- **Art. 2** — In this report, the Moderator may present:
    - Criticisms
    - Compliments
    - Complaints or suggestions
- **Art. 3** — The Regional Captain Moderator has the duty to receive, analyze, and resolve the issues presented, acting with agility and fairness.
- **Art. 4** — In cases of serious disputes that cannot be resolved at the Captain level, it is strongly recommended that they be forwarded to the Administrators and Ministers or, if there is reason for punishment, that a representation for impeachment be filed in the Senate.
- **Art. 5** — This reporting procedure, although flexible, is essential for the continuous improvement of moderation.
- **Art. 6** — This act is provisional and subject to ratification, modification, or revocation by the Great Khan at any time.

Let it be published and complied with.  

Done on May 18, 2026.
