OFFICIAL MINISTERIAL ACT No. 12
Ministry of the Civil House of Internal Relations
Khanate – Server
Subject: Definitive Appointment of Captain Nazaryan as Captain Moderator of Brazil

Date: May 25, 2026
By the authority vested in me as Minister of the Civil House of Internal Relations, and in accordance with the organizational structure established by the Khanate Decree, I hereby decree the following:

Article 1 – Definitive Appointment
The user Nazaryan is hereby definitively appointed to the position of Captain Moderator of Brazil.
Article 2 – Grounds for Appointment
This appointment is granted in recognition of the excellent performance and consistency demonstrated by Captain Nazaryan, who has been consistently meeting the established targets week after week, maintaining a high level of activity, organization, and responsibility in leading the Brazil region.
Article 3 – Duties and Responsibilities
The Captain Moderator of Brazil shall remain responsible for:

Supervising and guiding the Moderators of the Brazil region;
Mediating internal conflicts within the region;
Ensuring compliance with moderation goals and quality standards;
Reporting directly to the Ministry of the Civil House of Internal Relations;
Overseeing the proper functioning and hierarchy within his region.

Article 4 – Term
This appointment is definitive and shall remain in effect until otherwise decided by the Ministry of the Civil House or the Server Owner, in accordance with Ministerial Act No. 11 and the Khanate Decree.
Article 5 – General Provisions
Captain Nazaryan is expected to continue performing his duties with the same commitment, dedication, and professionalism he has consistently shown, serving as an example to other Captains and Moderators of the server.

Issued by:
Minister Marescallus Zanella (Biaggioni)
Minister of the Civil House of Internal Relations
Khanate – Server
