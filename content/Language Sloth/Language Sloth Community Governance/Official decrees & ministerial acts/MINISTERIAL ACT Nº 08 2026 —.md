**MINISTERIAL ACT Nº 08/2026** **(With Immediate Effect)**

**The Minister of Civil Affairs and Internal Relations**, in use of the powers granted by **Khanate Decree Nº 001/2026**, Article 4,

**MAKES KNOWN** to all Administrators, Captain Moderators and Staff members:

**Art. 1º** – Captain **Sr. Meow** is hereby transferred to the **Regional Captaincy of Southern Europe**, a position that was previously vacant. This transfer is made **at Captain Sr. Meow’s own request**.

**Art. 2º** – Considering personal request, merit, and recognition of work performed, the **Regional Captaincy of Brazil** is hereby temporarily transferred to Moderator **Nazaryan**. Justification: Nazaryan reached the weekly activity goal **twice in a single day** and has already hit the goal again in less than two days of the week, demonstrating a high level of activity and commitment.

**Art. 3º** – The transfer of the Brazil Captaincy to Moderator Nazaryan is of a **temporary nature** and may be made **permanent** in the future, at the discretion of the Great Khan or the Chancellor.

**Art. 4º** – Should the Great Khan or the Chancellor deem another candidate more suitable for the Brazil Captaincy, they may appoint a different person, regardless of this temporary appointment.

**Art. 5º** – Captains transferred under this Act shall perform their duties in accordance with **Ministerial Act Nº 05/2026** and other applicable regulations.

**Let it be published and complied with.**

**Done on** May 19, 2026.
