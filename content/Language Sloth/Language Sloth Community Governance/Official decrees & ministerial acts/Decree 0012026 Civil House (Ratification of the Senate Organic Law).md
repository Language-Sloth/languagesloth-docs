## Decree No. 001/2026 — Civil House

**OFFICIAL DECREE**  

**Khanate of the Great Khan**  

**1st Act of the Minister of the Civil House**  

**Ratification of the New Organic Law of the Senate**

I, **Marescallus Zanella (Biaggioni)**, Minister of the Civil House of Internal Relations, duly empowered by the Great Khan, **DECREE**:

- **Article 1** — The changes and updates contained in the New Organic Law of the Senate are hereby officially ratified and approved.
- **Article 2** — This Decree enters into force on **May 20, 2026**.
- **Article 3** — The President of the Senate shall have a period of two (2) days, counting from May 20, 2026, to formally notify all Senators of the full content of the New Organic Law and of this Decree.
- **Article 4** — All Senators and administrative bodies of the Khanate shall comply with the provisions of the New Organic Law from the date of its entry into force.

**LET IT BE FULFILLED**  

Issued on the 18th day of May, 2026.

Signed and sealed:

- Chancellor of the Great Khan (signature on record)
- For and on behalf of His Eminence the Great Khan (signature on record)
