**MINISTERIAL ACT Nº 10/2026** **(With Immediate Effect) - CORRECTED VERSION**

**The Minister of Civil Affairs and Internal Relations**, in use of the powers granted by **Khanate Decree Nº 001/2026**, especially Article 4,

**MAKES KNOWN** to all Administrators, Captain Moderators and Staff members:

**Art. 1º** – Moderator **Gregg** is hereby appointed as **Captain of the Regional Captaincy of Eastern Europe**, on a **provisional** basis.

**Art. 2º** – Moderator **@Krakow** is hereby appointed as **Captain of the Regional Captaincy of Turkey**.

**Art. 3º** – The appointment of Captain Gregg to Eastern Europe is of a **provisional** nature and remains subject to ratification, modification or revocation by the Great Khan at any time.

**Art. 4º** – The Captains appointed under this Act shall perform their duties in accordance with **Ministerial Act Nº 05/2026** (Moderation Code of Conduct) and other applicable regulations of the Khanate.

**Art. 5º** – This appointment enters into force **immediately** upon publication.

**Let it be published and complied with.**

**Done on** May 20, 2026.
