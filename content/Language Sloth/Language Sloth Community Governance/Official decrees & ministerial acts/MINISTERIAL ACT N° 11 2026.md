Subject: Guidelines on Moderation Hierarchy, Reporting Structure, and Restrictions on Unilateral Demotions

Date: May 25, 2026 By the authority vested in me as Minister of the Civil House of Internal Relations, and in accordance with the organizational structure established by the Khanate Decree, I hereby issue the following: 
Article 1 – Reporting Hierarchy
All Moderators must, preferably, report directly to the Captain Moderator of their respective region. It is strongly recommended that the Administration refrain from directly handling moderation cases that fall under the responsibility of the Captain Moderators, in order to respect the established chain of command and ensure proper regional oversight. 
Article 2 – Resolution of Doubts
In case of any doubts, questions, or issues related to moderation activities, Moderators shall first seek guidance from their respective Captain Moderator before escalating the matter to higher administration levels. 
Article 3 – Prohibition of Unilateral Demotions for Low Productivity It is hereby strictly prohibited for any individual Administrator or Minister to unilaterally demote a Moderator solely on the grounds of low productivity or isolated performance metrics. Demotions based on productivity may only be carried out through a collective decision involving:

The Server Owner, or No demotion of this nature shall be executed by a single authority without the required collective deliberation.

Article 4 – Purpose This Act aims to:

Strengthen the regional Captain Moderator structure; 
Ensure clear communication channels; 
Prevent arbitrary or unilateral decisions regarding demotions;
Promote a more organized, fair, and hierarchical moderation system.

Article 5 – Entry into Force This Ministerial Act enters into force immediately upon publication and must be observed by all members of the moderation team and administration. 
Any previous provision that conflicts with this Act is hereby revoked.
