## Ministerial Act No. 02/2026

**THE MINISTER OF CIVIL AFFAIRS AND INTERNAL RELATIONS AND EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF THE KHANATE**, in exercise of the powers granted by the Great Khan, **HEREBY DECLARES** that the following order of vicariate is established in case of the Great Khan’s absence:

- **Article 1** — In the event of the Great Khan’s self-declared absence, the Chancellor shall act as Vicar for the entire period of absence.
- **Article 2** — The Vicar shall enjoy all legal prerogatives and powers of the Great Khan during this period and must exercise them with loyalty and in the best interest of the Khanate.
- **Article 3** — The Great Khan may, at any time, suspend the effects of the vicariate, in whole or in part. All acts performed under the vicariate shall be considered derogated as soon as the Great Khan returns to functions.
- **Article 4** — In the simultaneous absence of both the Great Khan and the Chancellor, the function of Vicar shall be exercised successively by:
    - I. The Consul
    - II. The Minister of Civil Affairs and Internal Relations
    - III. The Minister of Justice
- **Article 5** — This Ministerial Act enters into force on the date of its publication.

Let it be published, registered, and complied with.  

Issued on May 18, 2026.

**Marescallus Zanella (Biaggioni)**  

Minister of Civil Affairs and Internal Relations  

Executive Officer of the Khanate
