> [!info] 📜 Ministerial Act 06/2026
> **Document type:** Ministerial Act (Immediate effect)
> **Number:** 06/2026
> **Subject:** Creation of new Regional Captaincies of Moderation

The Minister of Civil Affairs and Internal Relations, in use of the powers granted by Khanate Decree No. 001/2026 (Article 4), **MAKES KNOWN** to all Administrators, Captain Moderators, and Staff members:

- **Art. 1** — The following Regional Captaincies of Moderation are hereby created, with open positions to be filled:
    - Regional Captaincy of Japan
    - Regional Captaincy of the British Isles
- **Art. 2** — The positions above shall be filled according to the following mandatory technical criteria:
    - Fluently speak the language of the region (Japanese for the Japan Captaincy, English for the British Isles Captaincy)
    - Be an active user with good participation in the community
    - Be a trustworthy person, with a history of appropriate conduct and loyalty to the community
- **Art. 3** — The appointment of Captain Moderators for the regions created in this act may be carried out by any Administrator or higher-ranking position, provided that the nominee fully meets the criteria established in Article 2.
- **Art. 4** — The appointed Captains shall have the same duties and responsibilities provided for in Ministerial Act No. 05/2026, including coordinating the Moderators of their region and receiving weekly reports.
- **Art. 5** — This act is provisional and subject to ratification, modification, or revocation by the Great Khan at any time.

Let it be published and complied with.  

Done on May 19, 2026.
