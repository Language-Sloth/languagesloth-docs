
MINISTERIAL ACT Nº 09/2026
(With Immediate Effect)
The Minister of Civil Affairs and Internal Relations,
in use of the powers granted by Khanate Decree Nº 001/2026, Article 4,
MAKES KNOWN to all Administrators, Captain Moderators and Staff members:
Art. 1º – The Regional Captaincy of Quebec-France is hereby created, with its administrative headquarters in Montreal, responsible for serving the entire Francophone community of the server (France, Quebec, Belgium, French-speaking Switzerland, and other French-speaking regions).
Art. 2º – Moderator @UpsetSquirrel s hereby appointed as Captain of the Quebec-France Captaincy, with responsibility to coordinate, supervise, and oversee the Moderators and Francophone members of the server.
Art. 3º – The Regional Captaincy of China is hereby created, with the position currently vacant and to be filled in the future, in accordance with the established technical criteria and after superior deliberation.
Art. 4º – The measures provided for in this Act were defined after discussion and deliberation with the Consul, in alignment with the server’s moderation expansion and organization strategy.
Art. 5º – Captains appointed or to be appointed under this Act shall perform their duties in accordance with Ministerial Act Nº 05/2026 and other applicable regulations.
Let it be published and complied with.
Done on May 19, 2026.
