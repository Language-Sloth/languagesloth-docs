
**Document type:** Ministerial Act (With Immediate Effect)  
**Number:** Nº 14/2026  
**Subject:** Establishment of the Major Moderator Position and Appointment of Initial Officeholders

Date: May 30, 2026
By virtue of the authority vested in me as Minister of the Civil House of Internal Relations, and in accordance with the organizational framework established by the Khanate Decree, I hereby decree as follows:
Article 1 – Establishment of the Position The position of Major Moderator is hereby officially established within the organizational structure of the Khanate.
Article 2 – Hierarchical Position and Direct Accountability The Major Moderator shall hold a position superior to the Regional Captaincies with regard to informative supervision, support coordination, and the preparation of reports.
Major Moderators shall be directly accountable and subordinate to the Minister of the Civil House of Internal Relations, to whom they shall report exclusively.
The Major Moderator shall not exercise direct hierarchical authority over Captain Moderators for the purposes of punishment or removal.
Article 3 – Duties and Responsibilities The Major Moderator shall be responsible for preparing detailed and impartial reports on the performance of Captain Moderators and Moderators; for coordinating and training Moderators in the absence or unavailability of the responsible Captain Moderator; for temporarily covering the duties of a Captain Moderator when the latter is absent, thereby ensuring continuity of regional operations; for privately and discreetly correcting Captain Moderators when necessary, without causing public embarrassment; for de-escalating minor conflicts before they are escalated to the Senate; and for providing information and reports directly to the Ministry of the Civil House of Internal Relations.
Article 4 – Justification for the Creation of the Position and Appointments The creation of the Major Moderator position is justified by the need to strengthen the moderation structure through a dedicated support layer capable of addressing recurring challenges, including ego-related conflicts between Moderators and Captains, gaps in responsibility and training, occasional absences of Captain Moderators, and the overload of minor disputes reaching the Senate.
This position was established to provide proactive coordination, training, temporary coverage, and discreet correction, with the aim of improving efficiency, professionalism, and stability within the moderation system. Based on these criteria, the following users are hereby appointed as Major Moderators: @Nazaryan, @Menma and @UpsetSquirrel Their appointment is based on their demonstrated maturity, reliability, impartial judgment, and consistent commitment to the server’s standards, making them suitable to perform the sensitive duties of this position.
Article 5 – Presumption of Veracity Reports prepared by Major Moderators shall enjoy a presumption of veracity and shall be considered reliable and authoritative for decisions concerning the demotion, promotion, recognition, or evaluation of Captain Moderators and Moderators. Such reports may serve as primary evidence in administrative decision-making processes.
Article 6 – Limits of Authority The Major Moderator shall not hold general administrative or punitive power over staff members. 
As a general rule, corrective measures may be applied directly in a private and professional manner, while punitive measures against staff may only be applied with the express approval of the Administration or the Ministry of the Civil House of Internal Relations.
Exception – Temporary Censorship: In extreme cases where a Captain Moderator or Moderator clearly demonstrates lack of respect, the Major Moderator may apply temporary censorship, provided there is just cause and sufficient evidence. Such measures must be punctual and applied for the minimum time necessary, preferably following the order of warning, timeout, and mute as a last resort. All temporary measures must be immediately reported to the Ministry of the Civil House of Internal Relations for review. Sole Paragraph: The Major Moderator may apply sanctions to regular server members (non-staff users), in accordance with the established rules.
Article 7 – Entry into Force This Ministerial Act shall enter into force immediately upon its publication.
