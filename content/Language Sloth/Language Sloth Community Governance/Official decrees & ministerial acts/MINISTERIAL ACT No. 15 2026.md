**Subject:** Appointment of Major Moderator and Provisions Regarding the Accumulation of Positions

**Date:** June 1, 2026

By virtue of the authority vested in me as Minister of the Civil House of Internal Relations, and in accordance with previous Ministerial Acts, particularly Ministerial Act No. 14, I hereby decree as follows

### **Article 1 – Appointment**

The user **@itz_sr_meow** is hereby appointed to the position of **Major Moderator**.

### **Article 2 – Accumulation of Positions**

The accumulation of the positions of **Moderator**, **Captain Moderator**, and **Major Moderator** is permitted in practice. A single individual may hold more than one of these positions simultaneously, provided there is no conflict of interest or detriment to the performance of their duties.

### **Article 3 – Resignation from Captaincy**

The accumulation of the positions of Captain Moderator and Major Moderator is normally permitted and maintained.

However, if a Major Moderator who also holds the position of Captain Moderator resigns from the captaincy **with the intention of dedicating themselves exclusively to the position of Major Moderator**, the region previously under their command **shall become vacant**, and a new Captain Moderator must be appointed to ensure continuity of regional leadership.

### **Article 4 – Indication of Successor**

A Major Moderator who also holds the position of Captain Moderator **may indicate** a successor for their captaincy in the event of voluntary resignation.

This indication **is not mandatory**. If the Major Moderator chooses to indicate a successor, the suggested member must:

- Have been a Moderator for **at least 1 (one) month**; and
- Preferably present **good performance metrics** on the moderation panel.

The indication will be evaluated by the Ministry of the Civil House of Internal Relations, which may accept or reject it based on administrative convenience and necessity.

### **Article 5 – Entry into Force**

This Ministerial Act shall enter into force immediately upon its publication.
