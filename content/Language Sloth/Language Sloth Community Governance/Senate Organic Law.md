## Senate Organic Law

**Ratified version approved by the Great Khan**  

(Prepared at the request of the Great Khan for ratification by the Minister of the Civil House)

---

## Title I — Nature and Jurisdiction

- **Art. 1** The Senate is the collegiate body responsible for adjudicating impeachment proceedings against Moderators accused of:
    - Abuse of power
    - Serious disrespect toward the system and government of the community, including serious disrespect of the established hierarchy
    - Conduct incompatible with the position
- **Art. 2** The Senate has autonomy to judge proceedings, fully respecting the provisions of this Organic Law.

---

## Title II — Impeachment Proceedings

- **Art. 3** Every impeachment proceeding must have formal records (a complete file), containing:
    - A written, well-grounded complaint
    - Identification of the accused and the facts
    - Evidence submitted
    - Official notice to the Moderator
    - The defense of the accused
    - The report and votes of the Senators
    - The final decision
- **Art. 4** The President of the Senate may, on their own initiative or upon request, dismiss the proceeding for manifest irrelevance or manifest lack of evidence.
    - §1 When dismissing, the President must publish a brief report in the #senado channel explaining the reasons.
    - §2 The dismissal may be reversed by a simple majority of Senators within 48 hours.
- **Art. 5** The defense of the accused and adversarial proceedings should preferably take place in the #congresso channel.
    - §1 In serious cases, the President may determine that the defense and adversarial proceedings take place in a reserved channel (#senado or another private channel), ensuring transparency to Senators and preserving the dignity of the accused.
    - §2 The decision to change channels must be justified and communicated in #senado.
- **Art. 6** Senators are expressly forbidden from talking to the accused Moderator by DM about the proceeding.
    - Sole paragraph: all official interaction with the accused must be conducted in the channels defined by this Law.
- **Art. 7** Senate deliberations, votes, and internal discussions shall occur exclusively in the #senado channel.

---

## Title III — President of the Senate

- **Art. 8** The President of the Senate serves a term of 2 (two) years.
    - §1 The current term shall last at most until April 1, 2028.
    - §2 The President may resign at any time by formally communicating it in #senado.
- **Art. 9** Election and re-election shall occur by simple majority vote, with full quorum.
- **Art. 10** The term may be extended or maintained by the express will of the Great Khan.
- **Art. 11** The President may be removed from office for:
    - Inertia or unjustified absence
    - Incompatible conduct
    - §1 Removal shall be carried out exclusively by the Great Khan.
    - §2 The Great Khan may remove the President at any time due to force majeure, without the need for public justification.

---

## Title IV — Operational Rules

- **Art. 12** Senate deliberations, debates, and votes shall be conducted in the #senado channel.
- **Art. 13** The #congresso channel is intended primarily for the defense and statements of the accused.
- **Art. 14** The President of the Senate has the following duties:
    - Preside over sessions in #senado
    - Receive complaints
    - Decide dismissals and channel transfers (with notice in #senado)
    - Maintain order
    - Cast the tie-breaking vote
