## Server rules (quick guide)

<aside>
📌

**Purpose:** Keep this community safe, welcoming, and useful for everyone.

Rules apply to public channels, voice channels, DMs started via this server, usernames, nicknames, avatars, bios, and external links shared here.

</aside>

### Community standards

1. **No NSFW / graphic content**
    - No porn, nudity, sexual content, gore, or shock content.
2. **Respect & dignity**
    - No racism, sexism, hate speech, harassment, doxxing, threats, or targeted humiliation.
    - Keep swearing reasonable and context-appropriate.
3. **Controversial topics**
    - Politics, religion, and similar topics belong in the designated debate channel only.
    - Do not use profiles (name, bio, avatar) to provoke or campaign.
4. **No shameless plugging / spammy self-promo**
    - No unsolicited ads, repetitive promotion, referral links, or “drive-by” marketing.
    - Ask Staff first if you want to share a project, event, or opportunity.
5. **Privacy & consent**
    - Do not share personal information about others without explicit consent.
    - No recording voice chats or reposting screenshots without permission from everyone involved.
6. **No spam or disruption**
    - No flooding channels, excessive pings, reaction spam, or disruptive soundboards in voice.
7. **No impersonation**
    - Do not pretend to be another user, Staff, or a public figure.
8. **No begging for power**
    - Do not ask for roles like moderator/teacher. Use official applications when available.
9. **No drama farming**
    - Don’t stir conflict for entertainment. If something escalates, disengage and open a ticket.
10. **Security & common sense**
    - Never pressure anyone to download files, run scripts, or “verify” accounts. Social engineering = ban.

### Enforcement (fair process)

- Staff may **remove content**, **issue warnings**, **mute**, **kick**, or **ban** depending on severity and repetition.
- If you believe a moderation action was mistaken, open a ticket and provide context. Be respectful—appeals are reviewed.

### Legal & rights considerations (plain-language)

- **Common law principles (fairness):** We aim to act with reasonableness, consistency, and proportionality. Repeat or severe misconduct is treated more seriously than minor mistakes.
- **Fundamental rights (respectful baseline):** We protect the community’s basic interests—safety, privacy, reputation, freedom of speech and freedom from harassment. At the same time, we support good-faith expression in the right place and tone.
- **No “rights” to harm:** Speech that targets people (harassment, hate speech, threats, doxxing) is not protected here and will be moderated.
- **Due process mindset:** When possible, we explain what happened, which rule applies, and what to do next—especially for first-time or low-severity issues.
