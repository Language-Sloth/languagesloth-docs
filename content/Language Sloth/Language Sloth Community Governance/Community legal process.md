## Principles

- **Presumption of good faith:** everyone starts neutral; action is based on evidence.
- **Evidence-based enforcement:** no punishment without verifiable proof.
- **Proportionality:** consequences should match severity and context.
- **Transparency:** decisions should be explainable and consistent.

---

## 1) Reports & rule violations

When a rule is broken or a report is filed, Staff may take action.

**Report requirements (mandatory):**

- Provide **solid evidence** (screenshots, clips, logs, links).
- Include **context** (what happened before/after, dates/times if possible).

If there is **no evidence**, no punishment is applied.

---

## 2) Investigation

Staff may:

- Ask questions publicly in the appropriate channels
- Temporarily restrict someone (e.g., timeout/mute) to prevent escalation
- Collect additional facts before deciding

Decisions should be based on evidence and fairness — not assumptions.

---

## 3) Right to assistance (Attorney Mod)

Anyone involved in a case can request an **Attorney Mod** to assist.

Attorney Mods can:

- Help organize evidence
- Help write a clear statement/defense
- Help keep the process calm and structured

---

## 4) Outcomes (punishment ladder)

Typical progression:

- ⚠️ **Warning**
- ⏱️ **Timeout**
- 🔨 **Ban**

Severity may justify skipping steps (e.g., extreme misconduct).

---

## 5) Appeals & second chances

Banned users may join the appeal server to request a review.

To be considered, the user should:

- Explain their side clearly
- Acknowledge the rules
- Commit to behavior changes

If they cooperate and accept the rules, they may be unbanned.

---

## 6) Repeated bans

If someone is banned again after returning, the only way back is an **unban purchase ($10)**.

---

## Roles in the process

- 🛡️ **Moderators:** enforce rules in text and voice channels and handle routine cases.
- ⚖️ **Judge** (@makemeport): applies common law and makes decisions based on logic, natural rights, precedents, and guidelines (including bans, unbans, and complex investigations).

---

## Goal

This system exists to protect the community and ensure everyone is treated fairly.
